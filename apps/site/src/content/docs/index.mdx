<h2 id="home-faq">Common questions</h2>

<div class="not-content vl-faq">
  <details>
    <summary>Is my vault uploaded anywhere?</summary>
    <p>
      No. Parsing, auditing, editing, and conversion run in your browser or terminal. Network
      features (breach checks, redirect checks, mobile link lookups, AI) are off until you start
      them.
    </p>
  </details>
  <details>
    <summary>What does the breach check send?</summary>
    <p>
      Only the first 5 characters of each password's SHA-1 hash, using the HIBP k-anonymity API.
      Full passwords and hashes never leave your device.
    </p>
  </details>
  <details>
    <summary>What do AI features see?</summary>
    <p>
      Only item names and hostnames, and only when you trigger an AI action. Passwords, usernames,
      TOTP secrets, and notes are never included.
    </p>
  </details>
  <details>
    <summary>Can I open an encrypted export?</summary>
    <p>
      No. Encrypted exports, including <code>.kdbx</code> and locked JSON files, are rejected so
      nothing gets corrupted. Export an unencrypted copy, work on it, then delete it securely.
    </p>
  </details>
  <details>
    <summary>Will it change my original file?</summary>
    <p>
      Never. You review proposed changes, then download a new file. Edits can be undone, and audit
      reports mask every password.
    </p>
  </details>
  <details>
    <summary>Is it free?</summary>
    <p>Yes. vaultlint is open source under GPL-3.0, with no account and no telemetry.</p>
  </details>
</div>
